Our Mission

More Than Modelling

Ace Models Potchefstroom is built on a singular belief: that every young woman holds within her an extraordinary capacity for confidence, elegance, and leadership. Our academy exists to unlock that potential.

As a proud affiliate of Ace Models International, we bring world-class training methodologies to Potchefstroom — combining runway technique, pageant preparation, personal grooming, and deep confidence-building work into a transformative academy experience.

We believe that when a young woman walks with confidence, speaks with conviction, and carries herself with grace — her possibilities become limitless.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What age groups do you cater for? +
We welcome young women from as young as 5 years old through to age 21. Each age group has its own tailored curriculum and class times. Our programmes grow with your child — from our Junior Foundation classes right through to our Senior Models professional programme.
Do students need prior experience to apply? +
Absolutely not. We welcome complete beginners with enthusiasm and potential. What matters to us is a willingness to learn, a desire to grow, and a commitment to the journey. Our coaches are experienced in building confidence from the ground up.
How often are classes held? +
Core group classes are held weekly on Saturdays, with additional specialised sessions on Wednesday evenings (public speaking & etiquette) and Thursday evenings (pageant intensive). Private coaching is available by appointment throughout the week.
What is included in the monthly fee? +
The monthly programme includes all weekly group classes for your age group, access to our full age-appropriate curriculum, runway and movement training, basic etiquette coaching, and access to our student showcases. Our quarterly and annual programmes include additional benefits such as portfolio photography and competition preparation.
Do you prepare students for national pageants? +
Yes — pageant preparation is a cornerstone of our advanced programmes. We prepare students comprehensively for pageant competition including interview skills, evening wear, on-stage presence, and overall presentation. Our track record includes numerous national title winners and international finalists.
Is Ace Models Potchefstroom affiliated with Ace Models International? +
Yes. We are an official affiliate of Ace Models International, which means our training standards, curriculum, and competition pathways align with internationally recognised modelling and personal development programmes.
